## Research Experience

### Undergraduate Thesis / Course Project · Ongoing

**CFD analysis of fluid flow and heat transfer around confined geometries**

Under the supervision of Md. Abadur Rahman, Lecturer, Department of Mathematics,
Kishoreganj University. The project studies flow and heat transfer around
semi-circular cylinders and airfoil-shaped channels, including vortex shedding,
Reynolds- and Prandtl-number effects, and hybrid nanoparticles in Newtonian
fluids. Tools: COMSOL Multiphysics, ANSYS, and MATLAB.

### Independent Research Project · Ongoing

**Thermal comfort optimization and energy consumption analysis in enclosed spaces**

Developing a numerical model to study heater placement, cycle time, and
electricity cost under a fixed temperature boundary. The work also examines heat
loss through doors and windows and temperature decay.
