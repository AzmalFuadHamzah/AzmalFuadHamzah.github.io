## Overview

This ongoing undergraduate thesis / course project studies fluid flow and heat
transfer around semi-circular cylinders and airfoil-shaped channels.

## Questions

- How do Reynolds and Prandtl numbers affect flow and heat transfer?
- When does vortex shedding begin in the geometries being studied?
- How do hybrid nanoparticles affect heat transfer in Newtonian fluids?

## Methods and Tools

The work uses numerical modeling, meshing, and simulation with COMSOL
Multiphysics, ANSYS, and MATLAB.
