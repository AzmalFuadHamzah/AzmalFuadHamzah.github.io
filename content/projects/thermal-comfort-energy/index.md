## Overview

This independent project develops a numerical model to study thermal comfort
and energy consumption in enclosed spaces.

## Questions

- How can heater placement and cycle time be selected while maintaining a fixed
  temperature boundary?
- How do doors and windows affect heat loss and temperature decay?
- How can heating energy and electrical cost be reduced?
